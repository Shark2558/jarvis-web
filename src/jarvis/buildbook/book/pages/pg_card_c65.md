## In plain words
Emergency SOS lets you call for help from family or friends in one deliberate move. You hold a button, a short countdown runs, and JARVIS sends a fixed message with your location to your emergency contacts on Telegram, then opens your phone's SMS app filled in for you to press Send, and offers to dial your first contact or the emergency number (`src/jarvis/sos/service.py`). It is not an emergency service. The brain can never send one: your hold and the countdown are the confirmation, and the only SOS tool JARVIS has just tells you how it is set up (`sos_tools`).

## Example (made up)
Sunita is walking home at 21:40 when her ankle gives way on a dark lane. She holds the SOS tile on her phone; after the 10-second countdown her brother and two neighbours get "SOS from Sunita: I need help. My location at 21:40: a map link (within about 20 m). Sent by my JARVIS assistant." plus a pin on the map. Her SMS app opens with the same words ready, and a button offers to call 112. Her brother replies on Telegram that he is coming; JARVIS never reads that reply.

## How it works
In the console you hold the button for {{fact:src/jarvis/sos/service.py:HOLD_S}} seconds and a {{fact:src/jarvis/sos/service.py:WEB_COUNTDOWN_S}}-second countdown follows; the phone's tile or shortcut counts down {{fact:settings.sos_countdown_s}} seconds (`src/jarvis/sos/service.py`).
Only `POST /v1/sos/send` and the phone's signed door reach `SosService`, so no tool and no brain can start a send.
`message_for` writes every word from your own label, the time the phone fixed your place and a rounded accuracy; a test says it is a test and "I'm safe" carries no place.
`Limits` takes a slot in one transaction: a real SOS may go {{fact:settings.sos_per_hour}} times an hour and {{fact:settings.sos_per_day}} a day, and tests have their own separate budget, so tests never block a real one.
Each joined contact, at most {{fact:settings.sos_max_contacts}}, gets the message once and then a map pin, and each result is honest: sent, sent without the pin, failed, or not joined (`SosService`).
A repeated press id from the same hold gets the first answer and sends nothing again (`src/jarvis/sos/service.py`).
`AfterSend` writes one log entry and one audit line with counts only, and sends you a notice on every channel, waiting at most {{fact:src/jarvis/sos/after.py:AFTER_S}} seconds so a slow log never holds "sent".
Contacts join by pressing Start on a one-time Telegram link and leave with stop; `ContactHook` handles both before anything else in the Telegram front door looks at the message (`src/jarvis/sos/hook.py`).
`SosStore` keeps your label and contacts sealed in `sos`, join words only as a keyed hash, and a log of each send with no place in it.

## What it costs
Nothing in JARVIS: the messages go through JARVIS's Telegram bot and no AI writes any word (`src/jarvis/sos/message.py`). The SMS from your phone is charged at your mobile plan's rate, and only if you press Send yourself.

## When it fails
A refused or failed send still opens the SMS screen and the emergency-number button, and the phone uses the numbers it keeps whatever the answer (`src/jarvis/sos/service.py`). With no contact, SOS stays off. If the limits can't be read, a real SOS still goes, bounded by a counter in memory, while a test is refused (`Limits`). A press that reached nobody gives its slot back. Your SOS notice has no off switch, and no Telegram cap drops it (`src/jarvis/sos/service.py`). A broken notifier, log or audit never fails the send. While the app is locked only a real SOS goes, and the answer names no contact (`src/jarvis/api/sos.py`).
