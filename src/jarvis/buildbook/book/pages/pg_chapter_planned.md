## In plain words
This chapter lists what isn't part of JARVIS yet, so you know which parts you use today and which exist only on paper; every other page describes the code as it runs now. This release, {{fact:release.label}}, "Calls from your laptop", is built and has left the list: after your tap, JARVIS places a phone call from your own number through your laptop, speaks English and the Indian languages that pass its language check, and keeps a sealed record of the call.
Planned: next the voice build, then the home cameras with the first home vision uses, then the kitchen vision uses. Later: care, a fitness coach, smart-home extras, subscription releases, a Health and Wellbeing skill, a trading desk and deeper tracing. Left out of the calls release for later: calls while the laptop is off, a second SIM for forwarded calls, the WhatsApp route and a launch release. None of it is built, and plans change.

## Example (made up)
Priya opens the Book on her phone one evening and wonders whether JARVIS can already watch her front door. She opens this chapter and finds the home cameras second on the list, just after the voice build. So she doesn't hunt for them in the console. She turns instead to the Phone calls chapter, which tells her what a call from her laptop needs today, presses Play to hear it, and makes a note to look again after the next 2 releases.

## How it works
The architecture guide, `docs/architecture/JARVIS-Architecture.md`, gives every part of JARVIS a card and marks in capitals any part that isn't built yet, so a reader never mistakes a plan for a feature. Its roadmap section lists the next releases in the order the owner set, with what each one holds, and says plainly that nothing in it is built (`docs/architecture/JARVIS-Architecture.md`). The short tour reads its "next" and "after that" lines from the first two rows of that table (`docs/architecture/JARVIS-Architecture.md`). Each release has its own plan, saying what it holds and leaves for later: the plan for {{fact:release.label}}, `docs/plans/r18-calls.md`, holds calls placed after your tap from your own number through Phone Link on the laptop, the language check that decides which Indian languages a call may use, answering the calls you miss (off until you switch it on), a ready card for laundry calls, bookings added to your calendar, and each call's summary, transcript and recording in the vault. The guide's cards C74 to C78 call those parts built and joined, not yet deployed, so the pages that tell them describe them as built (`docs/architecture/JARVIS-Architecture.md`). The same plan names in its "Later" column what this release leaves out, so they stay on this list until a release takes them (`docs/plans/r18-calls.md`).
Planned: after {{fact:release.label}}, R5.2, the voice build with the microphone and listening fixes you asked for; R8, the home cameras' setup and the first home vision uses on the on-device vision engine, each starting after you confirm its cameras; R8.1, the next release in your order; R9, the kitchen vision uses. Later: R10 care and a fitness coach, R12 smart-home extras, subscription releases, the Health and Wellbeing skill, R19 a trading desk with the decision left for later, and tracing with OpenTelemetry. From the calls plan's "Later" column: a home call box so calls work when the laptop is off, a second SIM at home for forwarded calls, the WhatsApp Business route, which needs a registered business, calls while the laptop sleeps, menu keys pressed on Phone Link's own keypad if tones don't work, and a "Launch" release with this release's launch items. A follow-up to R18 adds the brain's check of each call language. From R6.4's "Not in R6.4", only when you ask: a Simple or Technical toggle, an interactive architecture map, print views and a guest-safe full book.

## What it costs
Reading this list costs nothing. What a release on it would cost is worked out in that release's own plan, before any work starts, and is not guessed here (`docs/architecture/JARVIS-Architecture.md`). The calls release did exactly that: its plan priced the line, the speech, the brain and the caps in rupees before anything was built (`docs/plans/r18-calls.md`).

## When it fails
A roadmap row goes out of date when plans change; the guide is updated with each release, and its change log at the end says what moved (`docs/architecture/JARVIS-Architecture.md`). A plan's own change log says what changed in it after approval, as {{fact:release.label}}'s records your answer to keep each call's audio as well (`docs/plans/r18-calls.md`). The roadmap's rows follow the order the Workshop relayed from your release plan, which itself is not in this repository, so a change there reaches this page only when the guide is updated (`docs/architecture/JARVIS-Architecture.md`).
