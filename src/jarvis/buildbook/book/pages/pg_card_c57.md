## In plain words
The Build Book is JARVIS's own record of how it is built. With every release it writes down its parts, the technology and libraries it uses, how they fit together, what each costs and where it falls short, then seals that book and the release's code in your vault, so you keep a true copy of every version you ran. You open it from the Book panel with your passkey and a fresh Google sign-in. Inside are the book's sections, a short narrated tour, a show you give a guest, and the full book: a page for every part, module, screen and outside service, written by the Workshop while it builds JARVIS and checked against the code. The full book has a page of its own, with contents and search, and its pages are grouped into chapters, each told aloud from a script the Workshop writes. JARVIS calls no AI to write any of it, so the book is ₹0 and there is nothing for you to approve.

## Example (made up)
On a Sunday a new version of JARVIS goes live. Within minutes its book is sealed, and the Book panel's release card turns to Sealed; Kept releases lists three versions. You open the book for yourself with your fingerprint, tap Read the full book, then Vault in the contents. The page says "Written by the Workshop · 24 Nov 2026", and a flowchart sits in How it works. You press Play, and JARVIS tells the chapter while the boxes light up. Later you show a friend, Neha, the short tour as a guest. Your other console tabs lock while it plays, and at the end you lift the lock with your passkey.

## How it works
At each release the generator builds the book from the code itself, with no AI: its sections, a copy of the source, the fact pack and the full book (`src/jarvis/buildbook/gen/write.py`). The Workshop writes the full book's pages and chapter scripts during development, and `compile_pages` checks each page against this release's facts and fills in its numbers; a page that is missing or fails a check goes in as the generator's plain text, so a page never breaks a build (`generator_page`). `compile_narration` adds the chapters to the same pages file, with the lines of each chapter whose script passes its checks. On every pull request CI runs the checks in `src/jarvis/buildbook/gen/pages_cli.py`, which refuse a page or a script that is missing, failing, or older than what it describes. Once the release is live, `BookSealer` seals its book, code, pages and flowcharts in your vault, keeping the newest {{fact:src/jarvis/buildbook/model.py:KEEP}} releases within {{fact:settings.book_max_total_mb}} MB, never at start-up (`src/jarvis/buildbook/sealer.py`). `open_flow` opens the book only with passkeys in force and a fresh Google sign-in, the proof bound to whether the session is yours or a guest's (`src/jarvis/api/book_show_access.py`). A session lasts at most {{fact:settings.book_session_minutes}} minutes and ends after {{fact:settings.book_idle_minutes}} idle ones (`src/jarvis/buildbook/sessions.py`). During a guest's show, `ShowLockMiddleware` holds your other consoles to the show's own routes until your passkey lifts the lock (`resume_flow`). `BookTalk` answers up to {{fact:settings.book_talk_questions}} questions a session from the book alone. `BookPages` reads a kept release's pages, chapters and search from its sealed files, for the main owner's own session only (`src/jarvis/api/book_pages.py`). Read the full book opens them on `src/jarvis/api/static/book-read.html`, the token kept in that tab alone (`src/jarvis/api/static/console-book-read.js`); search finds the pages holding every word you type, at most {{fact:src/jarvis/buildbook/writer/search.py:RESULTS_MAX}} (`find`). Play tells a chapter in the browser's voice, and `src/jarvis/api/static/console-book-fx.js` lights each box and connector as it is told. The old writer trial's records keep a Privacy row, "Book writer (old trial)", until you delete them (`OldWriterSource`).

## What it costs
₹0 for the book, its sealing, the full book, its search and its chapters, the show and the tour: the generator and the compile make no AI call (`src/jarvis/buildbook/gen/compile_pages.py`), a chapter is always told in the browser's own voice (`src/jarvis/api/static/console-book-chapter.js`), and the show speaks in that voice too unless you switch to the cloud voice in Book settings. A question in the talk-through is one ordinary paid AI reply, counted against your monthly limit (`BookTalk`). The sealed books share your vault's space, at most {{fact:settings.book_max_total_mb}} MB in all.

## When it fails
A book that can't be built never stops a deploy: the release goes live without it, and you get one notice (`src/jarvis/buildbook/gen/runner.py`). A page that is missing or fails a check reads "Not written yet" with the generator's plain text, and every other page still opens. A release sealed before v6.3.2 has no full book, and the reader says so (`src/jarvis/buildbook/writer/texts.py`). When the vault can't be read, the full book asks you to try again in a moment (`BookPages`). A chapter whose script is missing or fails a check is listed without its lines (`compile_narration`). A passkey lost during a guest show is recovered only by the emergency unlock, run with the server's own Google Cloud access (`src/jarvis/buildbook/unlock.py`). With No-AI on, or at the monthly limit, a question gets a fixed line and no call.
