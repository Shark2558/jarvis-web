## In plain words
This module lets JARVIS make phone calls for you from your own mobile number, through your laptop, and answer the calls you miss if you switch that on. You say who to call and why; JARVIS writes a call card and dials only after your press on it. It says first that it is an AI assistant calling for you and that the call is recorded, speaks English and the Indian languages that passed its language check, stays within the card and sends you an English summary. Before the first call you set up four things: the call desk on your laptop with VB-CABLE, Phone Link for calls, the new phone app with Allow calls, and the language check in Calls, Languages (`deploy/calldesk/README.txt`).

## Example (made up)
Nikhil wants his AC serviced. He types "call the service centre and book a visit on Saturday morning". The card shows the number ending 2290, "may agree: a visit Saturday 09:00 to 12:00; no payment", English and Hindi, 6 minutes at most and about ₹1. He presses Call and touches his passkey, because the number is new. His phone dials from his own number, the laptop carries the sound, and he follows the captions in the Calls panel. The centre offers 10:30; JARVIS repeats it back, the visit goes on his calendar with an Undo, and the summary, transcript and recording wait in Calls for 30 days.

## How it works
Every call starts with your press on its card: Call runs the gateway's irreversible call card every time, with no Approve all and no "don't ask again", and a number never called before needs your passkey (`src/jarvis/calls/approvals.py`). Your approval becomes one dial grant for that number and card, which the call engine spends just before the dial, after counting the day's limits (`src/jarvis/calls/limits.py`). The phone app gets the dial as a signed command, woken by a push that never carries the number, and dials from your SIM (`src/jarvis/phone/calls.py`). On the laptop the call desk checks VB-CABLE, Phone Link and that no Bluetooth headset is connected, makes the cable the microphone for that call only, and sends the other person's voice only while they speak (`src/jarvis/calldesk/session.py`). The engine hears them in the call's language, switches to another language the card allows when it hears one, and answers through the in-call brain, which has no tools; every line passes a guard that refuses OTP-like digits, PAN or bank details, links and agreeing to pay (`src/jarvis/calls/guard.py`). Speech is cloud speech picked by the language check, free first: Google inside JARVIS's free allowance, Azure's free tier if set up, Sarvam only with your yes (`src/jarvis/calls/join_speech.py`). During the call, Listen live plays both sides with English captions, Captions only is the default, Take over hands the call to you, Tell JARVIS passes on an instruction the other person doesn't hear, and Hang up ends it (`src/jarvis/api/static/console-calls-live.js`). Afterwards the audio, the transcript and the summary are sealed and kept {{fact:settings.calls_retention_days}} days unless you press Keep; if the other person objects to the recording, what was recorded is deleted (`src/jarvis/calls/records.py`). The Privacy panel lists them under Phone calls, with Export and Delete (`src/jarvis/calls/privacy.py`). A booking goes on your Google Calendar with Undo (`src/jarvis/calls/booking.py`). Answering missed calls stays off until you switch it on, and then only takes a message (`src/jarvis/calls/answering.py`). Calls opens from the dock where its tab fits without moving another tab, from Vitals' calls line, from a call card in the chat and from its own link (`src/jarvis/api/static/console-calls-vitals.js`).

## What it costs
The phone line is your own plan and the call desk is free (`deploy/calldesk/README.txt`). Speech is ₹0 inside the free allowances, and the brain and the summary are paid calls on the fast tier (`src/jarvis/calls/cards.py`). By default one call may spend at most ₹{{fact:settings.calls_call_cap_inr}} and last {{fact:settings.calls_max_minutes}} minutes, and all calls together ₹{{fact:settings.calls_month_cap_inr}} a month, inside your overall monthly limit (`src/jarvis/config_calls.py`). Each card shows its estimate before you press Call, and at a cap JARVIS says goodbye and ends the call (`src/jarvis/calls/limits.py`).

## When it fails
Anything missing at the laptop or the phone is named on the card, and nothing is dialled (`src/jarvis/calls/join.py`). A call outside the limits is refused in plain words: {{fact:src/jarvis/calls/models.py:CALLS_PER_DAY}} calls a day, {{fact:src/jarvis/calls/models.py:CALLS_PER_NUMBER_PER_DAY}} to one number, {{fact:src/jarvis/calls/models.py:NEW_NUMBERS_PER_DAY}} new numbers, none at night, and never an emergency, premium-rate or international number (`src/jarvis/calls/models.py`). With the call desk stopped, JARVIS can't call from the laptop (`deploy/calldesk/README.txt`). A restart ends a call in memory and leaves no approval to dial on (`src/jarvis/calls/hub.py`). The laptop speech kit joins the calls' speech once you set it up (`src/jarvis/api/r18_wiring.py`), but an option passes the language check only with a voice of its own, which this first kit lacks, so every call still uses cloud speech; the brain's own check of each language is not built, so it never fails a language (`src/jarvis/calls/langcheck/runner.py`).
