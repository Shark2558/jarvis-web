## In plain words
This card gathers the fixes for problems you reported, in groups (`src/jarvis/fixes/ports.py`). Answers: JARVIS never replies with an empty filler any more, and a batch of calendar events goes on one approval card with Undo all. Voice and files: it shows "Heard / Understood as" when it corrected a misheard word, and spreadsheets, slides, pictures, zips and folders are now accepted as attachments (`src/jarvis/documents/limits.py`). Photos and faces: your home laptop groups faces in your photos so that naming a person once, with consent, is enough (`src/jarvis/facegroups/service.py`). Pictures: JARVIS researches a product, place or label in a picture, but never turns a face into a name.

## Example (made up)
Nisha asks JARVIS to put her 12-week yoga course in the calendar, Tuesdays and Thursdays at 07:00. One card lists all 24 sessions; she approves and they are made five at a time. In week 3 she skips a Thursday; after the session JARVIS asks once, and on "skipped" one card moves that session and every later one to the next free slots. Later she says "send the budget sheet to Ravi"; JARVIS heard "budget sheep", shows "Heard / Understood as", and fixes it from the name of the file she attached.

## How it works
`output_cap` lets a turn that works with tools or files write up to {{fact:settings.work_output_tokens}} tokens; an answer the cap cut off is asked for once more, and if it is still empty `reply_text` gives one honest fixed line about what happened (`src/jarvis/core/finish.py`).
The batch tool puts up to {{fact:settings.calendar_batch_max}} events on one card that always asks, then creates only what is missing, so a retry never makes an event twice (`src/jarvis/series/batch.py`).
`PlanBook` remembers the batch as a plan you follow, and the series service asks about each session once after it ends, follows the moves you make by hand and re-plans a skipped one on one card (`src/jarvis/series/service.py`).
`read_inbox` counts every unread message by kind from Gmail's own count and withholds codes and bank messages.
For a spoken message, `UnderstandService` applies fixed rules first, then, only when unsure and with the AI on, one fast check that `acceptable` may still throw away: no number, date, name or action word can change.
`AttachPolicy` lets one message carry up to {{fact:settings.attach_max_files}} files, and `src/jarvis/documents/sheets.py` reads spreadsheet values without hidden sheets.
The photo import reads up to {{fact:settings.photos_max_picked}} picked Google Photos in the background, and the laptop groups unknown faces on the laptop only; naming a group runs the Faces consent check first (`src/jarvis/facegroups/service.py`).
`BrainEyes` shows a picture only to a brain that can see and doesn't train, and `check_name` and `check_query` stop a name or web search the picture could have suggested unless you typed it (`src/jarvis/picture/turnguard.py`).

## What it costs
Most of these fixes are plain code at no cost: batches, check-ins, re-plans, the inbox count, the fixed rules for voice and face grouping (`src/jarvis/series/service.py`). AI use counts against your monthly limit like any reply: a longer working answer and its one retry (`src/jarvis/core/finish.py`), a picture's single look (`BrainEyes`), and the fast word check only when the rules are unsure (`src/jarvis/understand/service.py`).

## When it fails
A part whose setup fails is left out and the others still run (`src/jarvis/api/r11_1_wiring.py`). An answer still empty after its retry says so in a fixed line and is never stored for a model to copy (`src/jarvis/core/finish.py`). Without Google linked, no batch card is made (`src/jarvis/series/batch.py`). A file that is too big, hidden, locked or a zip bomb is refused in plain words (`src/jarvis/documents/limits.py`). If the word check fails the message goes as heard (`UnderstandService`). With the laptop off, the photo import and a face delete wait and say so (`src/jarvis/facegroups/service.py`).
