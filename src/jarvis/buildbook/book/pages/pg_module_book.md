## In plain words
The Build Book is JARVIS's record of itself, one book for each release, sealed in your vault. You open it in the Book panel with your passkey and read it in several ways: short sections on its architecture, technology, libraries, connections, workflows, limits, costs and decisions; a narrated short tour, or a show you give a guest; and the full book, on a page of its own with contents and search, a page for every part, module, screen and outside service, and a flowchart for each chapter and module. Each chapter of the full book is told aloud too, line by line, while its flowchart lights up (`src/jarvis/api/static/console-book-chapter.js`). Everything in it comes from the code when the release is built. The Workshop writes the pages and the spoken scripts while it builds JARVIS, and JARVIS itself calls no AI for the Book, so it costs ₹0 and asks you to approve nothing.

## Example (made up)
Ravi's JARVIS gets a new version on a Wednesday evening, and that night its book is sealed beside the two before it. On Thursday he opens the Book panel, touches his passkey and starts the short tour on his phone; it plays as flat cards, because the screen is narrow. On Friday he taps Read the full book, searches for "costs" and opens the Costs page, with its flowchart in How it works. He presses Play on its chapter, listens for four minutes and presses Stop. On Saturday the Continue card takes him back to the line where he stopped.

## How it works
`src/jarvis/buildbook/gen/facts.py` builds the release's fact pack from the code: every setting, route, constant and count a page may quote, so no number in the Book is typed by hand (`src/jarvis/buildbook/facts_model.py`). `src/jarvis/buildbook/gen/tour.py` fills the Workshop's fixed tour script, `src/jarvis/buildbook/gen/tour.json`, from those facts and times it at {{fact:src/jarvis/buildbook/tour_model.py:PACE_WPM}} words a minute; a guest's copy names no rupees (`_guest_safe`). `src/jarvis/buildbook/gen/pages_index.py` lists every page of the full book, and `src/jarvis/buildbook/gen/refs.py` gives each page the files it describes. The Workshop writes each page, flowchart and chapter script while it builds JARVIS; `src/jarvis/buildbook/gen/pages_cli.py` and `src/jarvis/buildbook/gen/narration_cli.py` show a writer what may be cited, check it and record what it was written from. `check` turns away a page that cites a file, name or setting that doesn't exist, types a number, or holds anything personal or invented (`src/jarvis/buildbook/writer/checks.py`). `compile_pages` turns the pages, with each chapter's script (`compile_narration`), into the release's full book, filling each number from the pack (`fill`); a page that is missing or fails a check is the generator's plain text instead (`generator_page`). `GET /v1/book` seals the running release for the main owner, and `BookPages` reads a kept release's pages, chapters and search back from the vault (`src/jarvis/buildbook/writer/serve.py`). `src/jarvis/api/static/console-book.js` is the Book panel: Open for me or Show a guest, the sections, the kept releases, the code download, Read the full book and Short tour. Read the full book opens `GET /book/read` in the same tab (`book_read`): `src/jarvis/api/static/console-book-read-app.js` draws the contents, one page with its flowchart (`src/jarvis/api/static/console-book-flow.js`) and Continue, and `src/jarvis/api/static/console-book-read-search.js` asks `GET /v1/book/pages/search` for the pages with every word you typed (`find`). A chapter's Play reads its lines from `GET /v1/book/chapters/{chapter_id}`, speaks them in the browser's voice, and `src/jarvis/api/static/console-book-fx.js` lights what each line names. The show page plays the tour or the guest show (`src/jarvis/api/static/console-book-show.js`).

## What it costs
₹0: the fact pack, the checks and the compile make no AI call, and JARVIS never asks an AI to write a page or a script (`src/jarvis/buildbook/gen/compile_pages.py`). Search reads the sealed pages, with no AI (`src/jarvis/buildbook/writer/search.py`). A chapter is always told in the browser's free voice (`src/jarvis/api/static/console-book-chapter.js`); the tour and the show use it too unless you switch them to the cloud voice (`src/jarvis/api/static/console-book-voice.js`). Only a question you ask after a show is a paid AI reply. The sealed books share your vault's space, at most {{fact:settings.book_max_total_mb}} MB in all.

## When it fails
A page that is missing or fails a check reads "Not written yet" in the full book, and every other page still opens (`src/jarvis/api/static/console-book-read-page.js`). A flowchart that won't draw is left out, and its page reads as before (`src/jarvis/api/static/console-book-flow.js`). A chapter whose script is missing or fails a check gets no Play, and its pages still read (`compile_narration`). A release sealed before v6.3.2 has no full book, and the reader says it was sealed before its full book was written (`src/jarvis/buildbook/writer/texts.py`). When the vault can't be read, the full book asks you to try again in a moment (`BookPages`). Too many reads or searches in a minute are refused for a moment (`src/jarvis/api/book_pages.py`).
