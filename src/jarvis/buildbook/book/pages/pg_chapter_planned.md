## In plain words
This chapter lists what isn't part of JARVIS yet, so you know which parts you use today and which exist only on paper; every other page describes the code as it runs now. This release, {{fact:release.label}}, "Your voice", is built and has left the list: a speech kit on your laptop, a voice session that tunes listening to you, your own "Hey Jarvis" and a speaker check.
Planned: next the home cameras with the first home vision uses, then the release after it in your order, then the kitchen vision uses. Later: care, a fitness coach, smart-home extras, subscription releases, a Health and Wellbeing skill, a trading desk and deeper tracing. Waiting from the voice release: a voice and Indian-language engines for the laptop kit, the speaker check's model file, and, before any public launch, your own wake-word model with freely licensed parts under it. None of it is built, and plans change.

## How it works
The architecture guide, `docs/architecture/JARVIS-Architecture.md`, gives every part of JARVIS a card and marks in capitals any part that isn't built yet, so a reader never mistakes a plan for a feature. Its roadmap section lists the next releases in the order the owner set, with what each one holds, and says plainly that nothing in it is built (`docs/architecture/JARVIS-Architecture.md`). The short tour reads its "next" and "after that" lines from the first two rows of that table (`docs/architecture/JARVIS-Architecture.md`). Each release has its own plan, saying what it holds and what it leaves for later: the plan for {{fact:release.label}}, `docs/plans/r5-2-voice.md`, holds the laptop speech kit, a voice session that compares listening engines on your voice and learns your mishearings, your own "Hey Jarvis" in two stages, and a speaker check on each device that never approves anything. The guide's cards C79 to C83 call those parts built and joined, not yet deployed, so the pages that tell them describe them as built (`docs/architecture/JARVIS-Architecture.md`). The plan's change log records what the build left out: the voice parts under a copyleft licence and the Indian-language engines that need PyTorch, so JARVIS's voice stays Google's and Indian languages use the cloud options the calls' check passes (`docs/plans/r5-2-voice.md`). The guide's known limits add that the speaker check's model slot is empty and that a launch needs your own wake-word model and a freely licensed source for the feature models it reads (`docs/architecture/JARVIS-Architecture.md`).
Planned: after {{fact:release.label}}, R8, the home cameras' setup and the first home vision uses on the on-device vision engine, each starting after you confirm its cameras; R8.1, the next release in your order; R9, the kitchen vision uses. Later: R10 care and a fitness coach, R12 smart-home extras, subscription releases, the Health and Wellbeing skill, R19 a trading desk with the decision left for later, and tracing with OpenTelemetry. From the voice plan: your own wake-word model, run by you on the laptop before any launch, and a speech project you name swapped in behind the same engine slots once its licence is checked. From R6.4's "Not in R6.4", only when you ask: a Simple or Technical toggle, an interactive architecture map, print views and a guest-safe full book.

## What it costs
Reading this list costs nothing. What a release on it would cost is worked out in that release's own plan, before any work starts, and is not guessed here (`docs/architecture/JARVIS-Architecture.md`). The voice release did exactly that: its plan priced everything at ₹0 a month, with the session's one Google comparison inside the free minutes, or its rupees shown first and asked (`docs/plans/r5-2-voice.md`).

## When it fails
A roadmap row goes out of date when plans change; the guide is updated with each release, and its change log at the end says what moved (`docs/architecture/JARVIS-Architecture.md`). A plan's own change log says what changed in it after approval, as {{fact:release.label}}'s records the parts the build left out and the one exception to its download rule (`docs/plans/r5-2-voice.md`). The roadmap's rows follow the order the Workshop relayed from your release plan, which itself is not in this repository, so a change there reaches this page only when the guide is updated (`docs/architecture/JARVIS-Architecture.md`).
