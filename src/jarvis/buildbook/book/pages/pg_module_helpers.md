## In plain words
This module is the set of everyday helpers that look ahead for you, nearly all of them free and plain code. The morning brief gives your day at a glance: calendar, unread mail, parcels on the way, family calendars, documents due, what the home camera noted and what needs you. The Helpers panel has tabs for Today (bills with a UPI payment made ready for your PIN, birthdays, your top priority), Home (chores, upkeep, weather, official disaster warnings, breach notices), Nudges (stand up, drink water, ideas for free time, each off until you switch it on) and Kitchen (recipe ideas that keep to your diet, rough nutrition, grocery spend). Groceries on your to-do list, family calendars and parcel tracking round it off.

## Example (made up)
At 06:30 Lata's brief says: 2 meetings, 1 parcel out for delivery, and the electricity bill due on Friday. At 09:00 the Today tab reminds her of the bill; she taps Pay, approves the card with her fingerprint, and her UPI app opens with ₹1,240 filled in for her PIN. At 15:10 an official heavy-rain warning for her district rings on her phone. In the evening the Kitchen tab suggests a dal from what is in her pantry that fits her low-salt diet.

## How it works
Cloud Scheduler wakes JARVIS through `POST /internal/brief/tick`, which makes your brief when it is due and then runs the background jobs (`src/jarvis/api/brief.py`); `require_scheduler` accepts only the scheduler's own Google-signed token. `src/jarvis/helpers/clock.py` runs the helpers' frequent jobs on the existing home watchdog clock and their daily jobs on the morning tick, so no new scheduler job is needed. Each helper message goes through `HelperSender`, which applies your nudge settings and caps, at most {{fact:settings.nudges_max_per_hour}} nudges an hour and {{fact:settings.nudges_max_per_day}} a day, then the alert policy's quiet hours. The brief's facts are written by code: parcels come from your order e-mails through `src/jarvis/brief/deliveries.py`, with no model at all. A background read of your Google data asks the gateway first, and only a permission row set to Allow reads (`src/jarvis/tools/background.py`). `src/jarvis/helpers/today/upi.py` writes a UPI payment link for your own app to open; JARVIS never pays, and the amount is capped at ₹{{fact:settings.bill_pay_cap_inr}}. `src/jarvis/helpers/house/weather.py` reads Open-Meteo for a rounded point near you, and `src/jarvis/helpers/house/sachet.py` checks NDMA SACHET's official warnings every {{fact:settings.disaster_poll_minutes}} minutes. `src/jarvis/helpers/house/breach.py` looks in your Gmail for breach notices from the services you signed up to, sending your address nowhere. `src/jarvis/helpers/kitchen/recipes.py` is the only kitchen part that calls a model, and only a brain that doesn't learn from what it reads; with the AI off it ranks your saved recipes instead. Nutrition comes from a bundled subset of public food data (`src/jarvis/helpers/kitchen/data/fdc_subset.json`). A family member's calendar is checked with Google before it is added (`src/jarvis/api/family.py`). A long list of new events goes on one card that lists them all (`src/jarvis/tools/calendar_batch.py`), and a plan you follow checks in after each session (`src/jarvis/api/fix_plan.py`).

## What it costs
The helpers cost nothing: Open-Meteo and SACHET need no key, the scheduler's jobs sit inside its free allowance, and nearly everything is plain code (`src/jarvis/helpers/sources.py`). A recipe idea and the brief's optional AI summary are ordinary model calls, counted against your monthly limit (`src/jarvis/helpers/kitchen/recipes.py`).

## When it fails
A helper part whose setup fails is left out and the rest keep running (`src/jarvis/api/r11_wiring.py`). Nudge settings that can't be read mean no nudge (`HelperSender`). A helper stopped by one of your permission rows says so in one fixed line at the top of its tab (`src/jarvis/helpers/paused.py`). A job that fails or runs out of time is reported "failed" and the others go on (`src/jarvis/ticks.py`). When the frequent clock has stopped, the Helpers panel says so (`src/jarvis/helpers/clock.py`).
