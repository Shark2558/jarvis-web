## In plain words
This module is your wardrobe in JARVIS. Every piece you own gets its own record, each shirt, each sock and each shoe, with where it is kept and whether it is ready to wear, in the laundry or out at the dry-cleaner. Laundry follows the basket, the wash, drying, pressing and the dhobi, and asks you when something doesn't add up. Ask "what should I wear tomorrow?" and JARVIS picks a look by fixed rules from your calendar and the weather, then shows it on a plain 3D figure shaped from your measurements. See it on me is an optional paid extra: for a garment you are thinking of buying, it makes one AI picture of you wearing it, after you agree to that picture's price.

## Example (made up)
On Wednesday evening Varun asks, "What should I wear tomorrow?" His calendar shows a client meeting at 11 and the forecast says rain. JARVIS suggests the navy trousers, the white shirt and the brown shoes, notes that the shirt needs pressing, and opens the try-on card with the grey figure already dressed. He swaps in the black shoes and taps Use this look. On Friday he shares a shop photo of a green kurta; See it on me shows the price, about ₹9, he confirms, and a picture marked "not a photo" appears beside the list of his own pieces.

## How it works
Each piece is in one of the states in `src/jarvis/wardrobe/states.py`, and only a ready piece may go into an outfit (`eligible`). Its history is an ordered, append-only list of events: a second record of the same movement changes nothing, and an older event never undoes a newer one (`src/jarvis/wardrobe/states.py`). The phone's wardrobe camera, a tap or a sentence sends events to `scan`, which corrects the device's clock and applies a retry only once. `labels_svg` prints sheets of small QR labels for identical copies, and `assign_label` sticks one on a piece. Every change from the panel goes through the same gateway, cards and audit as the AI's own call (`run_panel_tool`). `laundry` shows the basket, drying, pressing, loads and outside batches, and `laundry_review` the checks waiting for you; JARVIS never orders, pays or messages a shop (`src/jarvis/api/laundry.py`). `outfits` gives today's and tomorrow's plans with their reasons, decided by the fixed rules in `src/jarvis/wardrobe/styling/rules.json`: slots, dress codes, events such as Diwali or Holi, colours and rewear. `put_settings` picks the one calendar JARVIS may read and which of its fields, and an event's title is never stored (`src/jarvis/api/outfits.py`). When you ask what to wear, `src/jarvis/wardrobe/styling/tryon_card.py` adds one try-on card beside the answer, holding piece ids only, which the AI never sees. `src/jarvis/api/static/console-tryon-3d.js` draws the figure on your device from files checked against `src/jarvis/api/static/vendor/mannequin/m1/MANIFEST.json`; nothing about it is sent. See it on me starts only after your typed YES, bound to the provider, the model and where it runs (`src/jarvis/wardrobe/seeme/consent.py`). `Pairer` suggests pieces of yours to go with the new garment and never sends a private one. Each press passes every gate before spending: your consent, the AI on, the price you saw, your monthly limit and an atomic hold in `TryonCap` (`src/jarvis/wardrobe/seeme/make.py`). `GeminiTryOn` sends the photos to Google's image model and returns one picture, labelled a generated preview (`src/jarvis/llm/tryon_gemini.py`).

## What it costs
The catalogue, laundry, outfits and the 3D figure cost nothing: they are JARVIS's own code and records, and the figure is drawn on your device (`src/jarvis/api/static/console-tryon-3d.js`). Optional AI tagging of a piece's photo is capped at ₹{{fact:settings.wardrobe_ai_tag_month_inr}} a month. See it on me is paid per picture, its price shown first with {{fact:settings.tryon_tax_pct}} percent GST, and capped at ₹{{fact:settings.tryon_month_cap_inr}} a month inside your overall limit (`TryonCap`).

## When it fails
Without JARVIS's encryption key the wardrobe and try-on stay off, and nothing is stored in clear. An ambiguous name opens a check for you instead of a guess (`laundry_review`). A device without WebGL2, or a figure file that fails its check, shows a plain checklist of the look instead (`src/jarvis/api/static/console-tryon-3d.js`). A picture call that fails before Google bills releases the hold in full, one that may have been billed keeps it, and a retry never charges twice (`src/jarvis/wardrobe/seeme/make.py`). If the cap or your limit can't be read, the picture is refused (`TryonCap`).
